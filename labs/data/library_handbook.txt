FERNHILL COMMUNITY LIBRARY: MEMBER HANDBOOK

This handbook was written for lab 10 of the course. The library, its rules and its staff are invented.

OPENING HOURS

The library is open from 9 am until 6 pm, Monday to Friday. On Saturdays the doors open at 10 am and close at 4 pm. We are closed on Sundays and on public holidays. Every Thursday we stay open until 8 pm for evening readers. In August the Saturday hours are shorter and we close at 1 pm.

JOINING

Membership is free for anyone who lives, works or studies in the Fernhill district. To join, bring one document that shows your address, such as a utility bill or a bank statement. Your card is printed while you wait and can be used straight away. Children under 14 need a parent or guardian to sign the form. People who live outside the district may join for 15 pounds a year.

BORROWING BOOKS

An adult member may have up to 12 books on loan at one time. A junior member may have up to 8. The loan period for a book is 3 weeks. You may renew a book twice, by phone, at the desk or on the website, unless another member is waiting for it. Reference books, which carry a red spine label, cannot leave the building.

FILMS AND MUSIC

Films on disc are lent for 1 week and cost 1 pound each to borrow. Music recordings are free and are lent for 2 weeks. You may take no more than 4 discs at a time. Please check that the disc is in the case before you leave the desk, because we cannot accept an empty case as a return.

OVERDUE ITEMS

When an item comes back after its due date we charge a fine of 20p per day for each item. The charge stops growing once it reaches 6 pounds for any single item. Junior members are never fined. If you owe more than 10 pounds your card is suspended until the balance is cleared. We send a reminder by email two days before anything is due.

LOST AND DAMAGED ITEMS

If you lose an item you will be asked to pay its replacement cost plus an administration fee of 3 pounds. Pages that have been torn, soaked or written on count as damage. Please do not attempt repairs at home with sticky tape; hand the item to a member of staff and we will mend it properly. A replacement card costs 2 pounds.

RESERVATIONS

You can reserve any item that is out on loan, free of charge. We will hold it at the desk for 7 days after we tell you it has arrived. Items we do not own can often be requested from another library in the county for a fee of 4 pounds, and usually arrive within 10 working days.

COMPUTERS AND WIRELESS INTERNET

There are 14 public computers on the first floor. Members may book a session of up to 2 hours a day, and visitors may buy a day pass for 1 pound. Wireless internet is free throughout the building. The network is called FernhillGuest and the password is displayed on the noticeboard beside the stairs. Please use headphones for anything with sound.

PRINTING, COPYING AND SCANNING

Black and white printing costs 10p a page and colour printing costs 40p a page. The photocopier takes coins or a library card with credit on it. Scanning to email or to a memory stick is free. Staff are happy to show you how the machines work but cannot fill in forms on your behalf.

STUDY ROOMS

Three study rooms on the second floor can be booked by members for up to 3 hours at a time. Each room seats 6 people and has a whiteboard and a screen. Bookings open 14 days ahead. If you have not arrived within 15 minutes of your start time, the room is released to whoever is waiting.

THE QUIET FLOOR

The second floor is a silent area. Phones must be switched to silent and calls taken on the stairs or outside. Group work belongs in a study room or on the ground floor, where ordinary conversation is welcome.

FOOD AND DRINK

Cold drinks in bottles with lids are allowed everywhere. Hot drinks and food are allowed only in the cafe area on the ground floor. The cafe is run by volunteers and serves tea, coffee and cake from 10 am until 3 pm on weekdays.

CHILDREN

The children's library is on the ground floor behind the yellow doors. Story time for children under 5 is held every Wednesday at 10:30 am and lasts half an hour. A homework club for ages 8 to 12 runs on Tuesdays after school, from 3:45 pm until 5 pm. Children under 8 must be accompanied by an adult at all times.

EVENTS AND CLUBS

The reading group meets on the first Monday of each month at 7 pm; new readers are always welcome and the book is announced four weeks ahead. The knitting circle meets on Friday mornings. Author talks are advertised on the noticeboard and usually cost 5 pounds, with the money going to the book fund.

ACCESS

A lift serves all three floors and is beside the main entrance. Large print books and audiobooks are shelved together near the lift on the ground floor. A hearing loop is fitted at the main desk. Assistance animals are welcome in every part of the building; other pets must wait outside.

GETTING HERE

The library stands on Mill Lane, opposite the post office. Bus routes 7 and 22 stop directly outside. Cycle racks for 20 bikes are beside the east entrance. The car park behind the building has 30 spaces and is free for the first 90 minutes; after that please use the pay machine.

HOME DELIVERY

Members who cannot leave the house because of illness or disability can have books brought to them. A volunteer calls once every four weeks with a selection chosen to suit your tastes. To arrange this, ring the desk and ask for Margit, who coordinates the service.

LOCAL HISTORY ROOM

The local history room holds maps, parish records and photographs of the district going back to 1840. It is open on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 1 pm until 5 pm, or at other times by appointment. Pencils only, please: pens are not allowed near the archive. Gloves are provided for handling photographs.

DONATIONS

We gladly accept donated books in good condition, up to 2 bags per person per visit. We cannot take encyclopedias, magazines or textbooks more than ten years old. Books we cannot add to the shelves are sold at the quarterly book sale to raise funds.

VOLUNTEERING

Volunteers shelve returns, run the cafe, help with the homework club and deliver books to housebound members. We ask for a commitment of at least 2 hours a week. Anyone aged 16 or over may apply, and all volunteers who work with children are background checked.

LOST PROPERTY

Things left behind in the building are kept at the main desk for 28 days. Unclaimed umbrellas, gloves and scarves are then given to the charity shop on Bridge Street. Phones, wallets and keys are handed to the police station after one week.

YOUR CARD AND YOUR DETAILS

Tell us promptly if you move house or change your phone number or email. If your card goes missing, report it at once so that nobody else can borrow on it; you are responsible for anything borrowed before you let us know. We never share your borrowing record with anyone outside the library service.

CONDUCT

We want everyone to feel safe and comfortable. Members of staff may ask anyone who is abusive, or who repeatedly disturbs others, to leave for the day. A ban of longer than one week can only be issued by the library manager and may be appealed in writing.

CONTACT

The enquiry desk can be reached by phone on 01632 960 114 during opening hours. Email enquiries are answered within 2 working days. Suggestions for books we should buy are welcome; drop a slip into the green box by the desk.
