MONDAY MORNING
A cold start for most of us, with frost on the grass and a little fog in the valleys. The fog will lift by mid morning. After that it stays dry and bright, with light winds from the north. Top temperature seven degrees.

MONDAY EVENING
Cloud will build from the west this evening and the first spots of rain will reach the coast before midnight. Inland it stays dry until the early hours. Not as cold as last night, with a low of four degrees.

TUESDAY MORNING
A wet morning for everyone. The rain is heavy at times in the west and the roads will be slow, so leave a little early. It turns drier from the north by lunchtime. Winds are fresh from the south west. Top temperature ten degrees.

TUESDAY EVENING
The rain has cleared and the sky is clearing too. That means a cold night, with a touch of frost in the countryside. Towns and cities will stay just above freezing. Winds are light.

WEDNESDAY MORNING
A bright and breezy day. There will be plenty of sunshine in the east, and a few showers in the west, some of them heavy with a little hail. The wind is strong on the hills. Top temperature nine degrees.

WEDNESDAY EVENING
Showers fade away after dark and the wind eases. It will be a dry night for most of us, with long clear spells. Cold again in the north, with a low of one degree.

THURSDAY MORNING
A dry and settled day with high pressure in charge. Early mist will clear quickly. Then we can expect long spells of sunshine and only light winds. It will feel pleasant in the sun. Top temperature twelve degrees.

THURSDAY EVENING
A fine evening and a clear night. Mist and fog will form again in the valleys after midnight, and it may be slow to clear tomorrow. Take care on the roads first thing. The low tonight is two degrees.

FRIDAY MORNING
Fog is thick in places this morning, and some flights may be delayed. It should lift by eleven. The afternoon is dry with hazy sunshine, and it will feel mild for the time of year. Top temperature thirteen degrees.

FRIDAY EVENING
Cloud thickens from the south west tonight, and rain will arrive on the coast by dawn. It is a mild night, with a low of eight degrees. The wind picks up toward morning.

SATURDAY MORNING
A windy and wet start to the weekend. Rain will sweep across the whole region this morning, and gusts on the coast could reach sixty miles an hour. Tie down anything loose in the garden. Top temperature eleven degrees.

SATURDAY EVENING
The heavy rain moves away to the east and it becomes showery. The wind stays strong, and there may be thunder near the coast. Check before you travel, as some ferries are not running. The low tonight is six degrees.

SUNDAY MORNING
A much calmer day. There will be a few showers in the north but most places are dry, with sunny spells. The wind has dropped. A good day for a walk, but take a coat. Top temperature ten degrees.

SUNDAY EVENING
Dry and clear this evening. It will turn cold quickly after sunset, and there will be a frost by morning in most country areas. Winds are light. The low tonight is zero degrees.

THE WEEK AHEAD
The week ahead starts cold and dry, with frost at night and sunshine by day. From Wednesday it turns milder but more unsettled, with rain at times and strong winds in the west. There is a small chance of snow on the highest hills on Thursday night. The weekend looks drier.

THE COAST
On the coast today, winds are from the south west, fresh to strong. The sea is rough. There will be showers this morning, and visibility is good, but poor in the showers. High water is at four this afternoon.

THE HILLS
On the hills today, expect low cloud above three hundred metres and a cold wind from the north. There is ice on the higher paths. Take a map, warm clothes and a torch, and tell someone where you are going. The freezing level is at six hundred metres.

GARDENERS
A note for gardeners. There will be a sharp frost tonight, so cover young plants or bring them indoors. The ground is too wet to dig in the west. It is a good week to plant bare root trees, and to clean the greenhouse while it is quiet.

DRIVERS
A note for drivers. Fog is slow to clear on the main road north this morning, and there is standing water on the lanes after the rain last night. Use dipped lights, slow down, and leave more room than you think you need.

POLLEN AND AIR
The pollen count is low today. Air quality is good across the region, with a fresh breeze to keep things clear. The level of the sun is low, so no need for sun cream yet, but it is bright enough for sunglasses on the road.

SUMMER MONDAY
A warm and sunny day for all of us. There is hardly a cloud in the sky and the wind is light. It will be hot in the city this afternoon, so drink plenty of water and find some shade. Top temperature twenty eight degrees.

SUMMER MONDAY EVENING
A warm and sticky night to come. It stays dry, with clear skies, and the temperature in town will not drop below eighteen degrees. Open a window if you can. There is a small chance of a thunder storm by dawn.

SUMMER TUESDAY
The heat breaks today. Thunder storms will move up from the south this morning, with heavy rain, hail and a lot of lightning. Some places will miss them. Others could see a month of rain in an hour. It is cooler behind the storms. Top temperature twenty one degrees.

SUMMER TUESDAY EVENING
The storms clear away to the north this evening and it turns much fresher. A comfortable night for sleeping at last, with a low of twelve degrees. There will be some mist by the rivers at dawn.

SUMMER WEDNESDAY
A fresher and more pleasant day. Sunny spells and a light breeze from the west, with just the odd shower in the afternoon. It is a good day to dry the washing. Top temperature twenty two degrees.

WINTER MONDAY
A bitterly cold day. Snow showers will blow in from the east on a sharp wind, and a few centimetres could settle on the hills. In town it will be mostly sleet. Wrap up warm if you are heading out. Top temperature one degree.

WINTER MONDAY EVENING
Snow showers continue tonight, and ice will form on any roads that have not been treated. Some schools in the hills may be closed tomorrow, so check before you set off. The low tonight is minus four degrees.

WINTER TUESDAY
The snow showers ease and the sun comes out, but it stays very cold, and the snow on the ground will not melt. Paths are slippery. The wind is lighter than yesterday, which helps. Top temperature two degrees.

WINTER TUESDAY EVENING
A hard frost tonight under clear skies. It will be the coldest night of the winter so far, with a low of minus seven degrees in the valleys. Freezing fog may form by dawn. Please look in on older neighbours.

WINTER WEDNESDAY
Milder air arrives from the west today. Cloud thickens through the morning, and rain will follow in the afternoon, falling as snow for a time on the hills. The thaw begins tonight. Top temperature five degrees.
